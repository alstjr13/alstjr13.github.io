#### Objective
The main purpose of using least squares approximation, often called [Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_least_squares), is to find the best-fitting line (or hyperplane in higher dimensions) that minimizes the sum of the squared differences between the <em>observed data points</em> and the <em>predicted values</em> generated by the model. 

#### Usage
This method is foundational in:
- Statistics
- Econometrics
- Machine Learning
