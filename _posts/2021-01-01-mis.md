#### What is Management of Information Systems?
Management of Information Systems (MIS) by definition is computer-based system that provides managers with the tools to organize, evaluate, and manage department within an organization.
It's designed to transform raw data into useful information for decision making. (**turn raw data into insights that guide business strategies and day-to-day operations**)

#### Objectives:
- To support decision making, the purpose of MIS is to provide managers with information that helps them make better decisions.
  - Includes data on performance, trends, business forecasts
- Inproving efficiency, by automating data collection and report generation.
  - Improve operation efficiency: Tasks that would otherwise be manuual and time-consuming are done quickly and accurately
- Enhancing Coordination: MIS integrates different departments within an organization, ensuring that everyone is working from the same set of data. This enhances coordination between department and leads to smoother operations.
- Fascilitating strategic planning: MIS helps organizations plan for the future by analyzing trends and forecasting outcomes. It allows managers to make long-term decisions based on data rather than intuition.
- Resourse optimization: MIS provides insights into resource allocation, helping businesses use their financial, human, and material resources more effectively.
