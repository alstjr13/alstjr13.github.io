## The question

What drives post-consumer food waste at UBC's AMS Nest, and how could the student dining experience reduce it?

This team research project was conducted through the [UBC SEEDS Sustainability Program](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/seeds-sustainability-program).

## My role

**Data analyst and team lead** · January–May 2024

## Approach

The study combined online surveys, interviews, and naturalistic observation. We examined socio-demographic factors and food-waste behavior using qualitative and quantitative analysis.

## Recommendations

The report recommends exploring food sold by weight, a reusable program with financial incentives, and regular surveys to align menus with student preferences.

## Limitations & reflection

The study involved 62 participants. A larger sample and a more focused survey could help strengthen the findings. The estimated annual saving of CAD $500 was a proposal to the client, rather than a measured outcome.
