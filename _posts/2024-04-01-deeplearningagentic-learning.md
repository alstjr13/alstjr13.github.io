#### What is Agentic Learning?
Agentic learning refers to the ability of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) system to actively control and guide its own learning process, rather than passively learning from static data.
An agentic AI system can set its own goals and adjust accordingly based on environmental feedback, choose what to learn next, adapt strategies dynamically rather than following a fixed training protocol and self-improve over time through exploration, planning and self-motivation.
