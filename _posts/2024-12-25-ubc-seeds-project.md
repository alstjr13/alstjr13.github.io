### <ins>About</ins>
This page discusses a group project conducted as part of the [Social Ecological Economic Development Studies (SEEDS)](https://sustain.ubc.ca/programs/seeds-sustainability-program) program.

#### Project Summary:
This research, in collaboration with the SEEDS program at the University of British Columbia, aims to investigate the main drivers of post-consumer food waste in the AMS Nest building and propose practical solutions. Using a mixed-method approach including online surveys, interviews, and naturalistic observations, the study analyzed the impact of socio-demographic factors on food waste (includes qualitative and quantitative analysis). Based on the findings, recommendations for AMS Nest include selling food by weight, implementing a reusable program with financial incentives, and conducting regular surveys to adjust the menu according to consumer preferences and improve the dining experience for students.
