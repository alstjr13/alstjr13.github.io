#### Notes
- Question: **What does it mean? "Antecedents in the context of AI recommendation algorithms"**
  - Answer: The factors or conditions that precede and influence the system's performance, user trust, and adoption.
- Ordinary AI usage behaviour vs. AI Manipulation Behaviour
  - **Ordinary AI usage behaviour**: The behaviour which users press "Like" button on YouTube (for example), because they simply like the provided video content.
  - **AI Manipulation Behaviour**: The behaviour which users **intentionally** press "Like" button on Instagram (for example) to **intentionally** change the recommended contents.
    - 인스타그램 알고리즘 피드에 뜨는 추천 게시물들을 바꾸기 위해 하는 의도된 행동을 가르키는 것 같다. 
    - 온라인 쇼핑몰에서 내가 원하는 (좋아하는 부류의) 상품들을 추천하게 만들게끔 "좋아요" 버튼을 누르는 행동을 가르키는 듯.
    - Think of Content-based filtering 
      - On Netflix, the behaviour of clicking "Like" on "Action" type movies, because I simply like "Action" movies for example 
      - $\rightarrow$ I would get more "Action" movies popping up on "Featured Movies / Recommended Movies" on my front page
- AI manipulation behaviour focuses on the intent to manipulate the AI, acknowledging that such behaviours can often align with genuine feedback.

#### Objectives of this study
- If AI manipulation behaviours positively affect firms and individuals, it should be encouraged.
  - On the other hand, if it brings negative impacts, we must understand the antecedents that encourage manipulation in order to develop effective countermeasures.
