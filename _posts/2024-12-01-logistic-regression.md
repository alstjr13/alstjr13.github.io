#### Differences between linear regression and logistic regression:
- In logistic regression, we use a sigmoid for predicting. (i.e. $\bar{y_{i}} = \sigma{(\sum_{j}w_{j}x_{ij} + b)}$)
- We replace $\|\bar{y_{i}} - y_{i}\|^{2}$ with a different equation appropriate for classification.
